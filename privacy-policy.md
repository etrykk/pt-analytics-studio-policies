# PT Analytics Studio — Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft for review  
**Proposed effective date:** November 25, 2026  
**Version:** 2026-11-25  
**Developer and proposed operator:** Ethan Gudvangen (operator responsibility pending confirmation)  
**Release:** V4 Limited Real-Data Beta — invite-only

## 1. Overview
PT Analytics Studio provides business intelligence and predictive analytics for personal trainers and personal training businesses. This policy describes the proposed processing of information during the limited beta.

## 2. Information collected
Depending on the features used, the service may process trainer names, email addresses, authentication identifiers, workspace information and policy acceptance records; client names or identifiers, memberships, packages, attendance, cancellations, no-shows, payment amounts and transaction history, business-related check-ins and optional notes; and technical activity, security logs, errors and feedback.

## 3. Purposes
Information is processed to provide dashboards, business metrics, forecasts, retention and engagement analysis, business record management, security, troubleshooting, support and privacy-request handling. Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. This draft does not authorize unrelated cross-customer model training or marketing use.

## 4. Authorization and rights
Trainers must have appropriate authority to submit client information, provide applicable notices and obtain necessary permissions. Clients retain their applicable privacy rights. The service processes submitted information to operate and maintain the agreed service.

## 5. Authentication and hosting
The platform uses Google authentication and Render-hosted infrastructure, including PostgreSQL. Relevant providers may process information necessary for authentication, hosting and storage. Provider arrangements must be reviewed before real-data activation.

## 6. Security
The platform is designed to use authenticated accounts, restricted database roles, workspace isolation, encrypted database connections and backups. These safeguards require live verification before real-data activation. No system can guarantee complete security.

## 7. Restricted information
Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, sensitive medical histories, payment-card numbers, banking credentials, passwords, government identifiers, personal information about minors or information outside the approved business-data scope. Optional notes must not contain prohibited information.

## 8. Sharing
Information may be disclosed to authorized service providers as necessary to operate the service, address security incidents or meet applicable legal obligations. The proposed beta does not include selling personal information. Any additional uses or disclosures require review and appropriate notice.

## 9. Retention and deletion
Users may request access, correction, export or deletion through the privacy contact. Deletion requests require manual processing; submitting a request does not automatically erase records. Information may temporarily remain in backups or be retained where required by applicable obligations. Retention periods, backup expiration, identity verification and deletion procedures must be documented and approved before activation.

## 10. Limited beta
The beta is invite-only and may experience errors, changes or interruptions. Trainers should retain independent copies of important records. The Sample demo uses fictional data. The limited pilot reuses existing staging infrastructure; it is not a separate production database. Identifiable client records must not be submitted until activation is approved.

## 11. Updates
Updated policies may require renewed acknowledgment. Policy versions and acceptance times may be recorded.
