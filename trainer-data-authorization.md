## 1. Purpose
This agreement describes the proposed responsibilities of trainers and personal training businesses participating in the limited beta.

## 2. Authority
Participating trainers must be authorized to act for their business, have appropriate authority to submit client records, provide applicable privacy notices, obtain necessary permissions and respect clients' privacy rights. Agreement acceptance does not replace any legally required client permission or notice.

## 3. Permitted information
Approved business information may include client identifiers and names, memberships, packages, attendance, cancellations, no-shows, payment amounts and transaction dates, business-related engagement and check-ins, and appropriate business-related notes.

## 4. Prohibited information
Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, sensitive medical histories, payment-card details, banking credentials, passwords, government identifiers, personal information about minors or information shared without appropriate authority.

## 5. Processing authorization
The trainer authorizes processing within the agreed service scope for dashboards, business performance and engagement analysis, churn prediction, revenue forecasting, cancellation and no-show prediction, lifetime value estimation, reporting and recommendations. This authorization is limited to the service purposes described in the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Predictions
Predictions may be incomplete, inaccurate or affected by limited data. Trainers remain responsible for business decisions and should not rely exclusively on automated predictions when making decisions affecting clients.

## 7. Account security
Trainers must protect credentials, prevent unauthorized workspace access, avoid unauthorized account sharing, report suspected incidents and review the accuracy and appropriateness of submitted information.

## 8. Data management
Trainers may use available export and privacy-request features. Deletion and account closure requests require review and manual processing. Trainers must communicate relevant client privacy requests when necessary. Retention, deletion and access-revocation procedures must be approved before the real-data pilot begins.

## 9. Beta participation
Features may change or experience interruptions. Trainers should keep independent copies of important business records. Access may be suspended to address security, privacy or operational concerns. The pilot reuses staging infrastructure and is not a separate production environment. No identifiable client information may be submitted before explicit pilot activation.

## 10. Updates
Updated agreement versions may require renewed acknowledgment. Agreement versions and acceptance timestamps may be recorded.

## 11. Acknowledgment
Upon activation of the approved agreement, accepting the checkbox confirms that the trainer has reviewed the agreement and Privacy Policy and has appropriate authority to participate and submit approved information. Acceptance does not authorize prohibited data or waive clients' applicable rights.
